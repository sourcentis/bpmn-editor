@sourcentis/bpmn-editor

Standalone editor, ui: 'default', no provider and no persistence configured. Starts by importing sample.bpmn (a real BPMN 2.0 file, via editor.importBpmnXml()) so there's something to look at — edit it, drag new shapes from the palette, connect them, or use Import/Export in the toolbar to load/save your own file. Everything runs client-side.
